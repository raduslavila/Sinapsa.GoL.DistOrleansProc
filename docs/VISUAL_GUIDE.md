???????????????????????????????????????????????

Cell E (at chunk_0_0, position [31, 15]):
- Right edge of chunk_0_0
- Needs to count neighbor F from chunk_1_0

Internal Neighbors (already connected):
- A (top-left)
- C (top)
- I (bottom) from chunk_0_1

Cross-Chunk Neighbors (fetched during Advance):
- B (top) from chunk_1_0 edge
- D (middle) from chunk_1_0 edge  
- F (right) from chunk_1_0 edge
- J (bottom) from chunk_1_1 corner

Total: 7 neighbors correctly counted!
